# Better Terminal 0.2.0

Split panes, a quick terminal, find, workspace templates and a redesigned sidebar.

## What's new
- **Split panes.** ⌘D splits right, ⇧⌘D splits down. Drag a pane's header onto another pane to swap them. The grid shrinks to fit when you have fewer tabs and grows back.
- **Quick terminal.** Press ⌃` from any app (change the key in Settings → Terminal) for a small terminal in the corner of your screen, opened in your Finder window's folder or your current tab's folder. Esc hides it.
- **Find in terminal.** ⌘F, then ⌘G and ⇧⌘G to step through matches.
- **Workspace templates.** Save a layout, folders and startup commands, and open the whole setup in one click.
- **Command palette** (⌘K) now has Actions and Commands tabs. Press → for the last 30 commands from every terminal; ⏎ puts one at its prompt, ⌘⏎ runs it.
- **Shortcuts popup.** ⌘/ shows every shortcut. Type to filter, ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to run one.
- **Know when it's done.** A spinner while a command runs, a red dot when one failed, and a notification when a long command finishes in another tab.
- **Broadcast input.** ⌥⌘B sends what you type to every pane of the workspace.
- **Redesigned sidebar.** A calmer look with glowing selection, running counts and a status footer. When collapsed it opens as you move the pointer over it. ⌃⌘S collapses or expands it.
- **Themes and fonts.** Import iTerm2, Ghostty and kitty themes, and pick any monospaced font.
- **Right-click menu** in terminals (copy, paste, open link, find, split and more), underlined links, and ⌥⌘C to copy the last command's output.
- Middle-click a tab to close it; closing a tab that is still running a command asks first.

## Fixed
- Typing now always goes to the active terminal after a new tab, split or workspace switch.
- Right-clicking a pane selects it first, so Paste goes where you clicked.
- Switching tabs is instant.
- Imported dark themes stay readable when the Mac is in light mode.
- ⌘⏎ runs the highlighted command in history and in the palette.

## Good to know
- Tabs in the top tab bar can no longer be dragged (it moved the window too). Reorder and move tabs in the sidebar.
- The first time, macOS asks to allow notifications, and to let Better Terminal read your Finder folder for the quick terminal. Both are optional.
- The broadcast shortcut is ⌥⌘B. Settings → Shortcuts and the ⌘/ popup list them all.
- Full documentation: https://mohitkap12-a11y.github.io/Better-Terminal/docs/
