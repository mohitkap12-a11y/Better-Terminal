# Better Terminal 0.1.0

The first release: a fast, native macOS terminal that remembers where you left off.

## Highlights
- **Workspaces.** One per project, each with its own tabs, layout and folders. Switch from the sidebar or with ⌃⌘1–9.
- **Pane layouts.** Tabs fill a grid: 1×2, 2×1, 2×2 or any custom rows × columns. New tabs follow the layout.
- **Resume on relaunch.** Workspaces, tabs, layouts, folders, command history and the text on screen come back after a quit or reboot. A fresh shell starts below the restored text. Turn it off or clear it in Settings → Terminal.
- **History for every tab.** ⇧⌘H opens a searchable list: ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to insert, ⌘⏎ to run.
- **Move between panes** with ⌘ + the arrow keys; the pane you land on pulses.
- **Command palette** (⌘K) for workspaces, layouts, themes, settings and past commands.
- **Soft colour themes** (Default, Mist, Sage, Rose, Lavender, Sand, Nord, Black) in light and dark; System follows macOS.
- **Right-click copies** selected text.
- **Updates built in.** Stable by default, or choose Beta in Settings → Updates. Signed and notarized.

## Good to know
- Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later (Apple silicon and Intel).
- Programs that were running when you quit can't be revived; a fresh shell starts in the same folder.
- Your data stays on your Mac in `~/Library/Application Support/BetterTerminal`. The only network request is the update check.
