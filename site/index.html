Everything a modern terminal should do

Fast and native, with the organisation features you usually bolt on with plugins.

Workspaces

Give every project its own workspace with its own tabs and layout. Switch from the sidebar or with ⌃⌘1–9.

Pane layouts

Tabs fill a grid. Pick 1×2, 2×1, 2×2 or any custom rows × columns. New tabs follow the layout.

History for every tab

Click the clock (or ⇧⌘H), type to search, ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to insert, ⌘⏎ to run. Failed commands are one click away.

Move between panes

Hold ⌘ and press the arrow keys to jump to the next terminal. The pane you land on gently pulses.

Resume on relaunch

Quit and reopen: your workspaces, tabs, folders, history and the text on screen are right where you left them.

Command palette

⌘K jumps anywhere: workspaces, layouts, themes, settings and past commands.

Soft colour themes

Mist, Sage, Rose, Lavender, Sand, Nord and more, in light and dark. System follows macOS.

Updates built in

Stable by default, or switch to the Beta channel in Settings. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Local and private

No account, no telemetry. Your data lives in one folder on your Mac. The only network call is the update check.

Keyboard first

Everything has a shortcut, and ⌘K finds the rest.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Better Terminal is free and open source.

Where is my data stored?

In one folder on your Mac, ~/Library/Application Support/BetterTerminal: your workspaces, command history and, if you keep Resume on, the text of your terminals. Settings has a switch and a Clear button.

Does it send anything anywhere?

No telemetry and no account. The only network request is the update check against GitHub.

What does resume restore?

Workspaces, tabs, layouts, folders, history and the text on screen. Programs that were running can’t be revived, so a fresh shell starts below the restored text.

Which shells work?

zsh, bash and fish, with history capture built in. Any other shell still runs.

How do I get Beta builds?

Settings ▸ Updates ▸ Update channel ▸ Beta. Stable is the default.
