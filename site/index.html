Everything a modern terminal should do

Fast and native, with the organisation features you usually bolt on with plugins.

Workspaces

Give every project its own workspace with its own tabs and layout. Drag to reorder, drop a tab on a workspace to move it, right-click to rename. Switch with ⌃⌘1–9.

Pane layouts

Tabs fill a grid. Pick 1×2, 2×1, 2×2 or any custom rows × columns. New tabs follow the layout.

History for every tab

Click the clock (or ⇧⌘H), type to search, ↑↓ to move, ⏎ to insert, ⌘⏎ to run. Failed commands are one click away.

Move between panes

Hold ⌘ and press the arrow keys to jump to the next terminal. The pane you land on gently pulses.

Resume on relaunch

Quit and reopen: your workspaces, tabs, folders, history and the text on screen are right where you left them.

Command palette

⌘K jumps anywhere: workspaces, layouts, themes, settings and past commands.

Soft colour themes

Mist, Sage, Rose, Lavender, Sand, Nord and more, in light and dark. System follows macOS.

Updates built in

Stable by default, or switch to the Beta channel in Settings. Signed and notarized by Apple.

Local and private

No account, no telemetry. Your data lives in one folder on your Mac. The only network call is the update check.

Split panes

⌘D splits right, ⇧⌘D splits down. The new pane opens in the same folder, and the layout grows to fit. Drag a pane header onto another pane to swap them.

Find in terminal

⌘F searches the scrollback with highlighted matches. ⌘G and ⇧⌘G step through them.

Know when it’s done

A spinner while a command runs, a red dot when one failed, and a notification when a long command finishes in another tab.

Workspace templates

Save a setup (layout, folders, startup commands) and open it again in one click.

Search every command

⌘K searches the history of all terminals. Press → for the last 30 commands across everything, and ⏎ to put one at its prompt.

Broadcast input

Type once, send to every pane of a workspace. Handy for servers. ⌥⌘I turns it on and off.

Quick terminal

A global key drops a terminal down from the top of the screen, from any app. Press it again to hide.

Bring your own colours

Import iTerm2 .itermcolors, Ghostty and kitty themes, and pick any monospaced font.

Links and a real right-click menu

Links underline when you hover them. Right-click for copy, paste, open link, find, split and more.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Better Terminal is free and open source.

Where is my data stored?

In one folder on your Mac, ~/Library/Application Support/BetterTerminal: your workspaces, command history and, if you keep Resume on, the text of your terminals. Settings has a switch and a Clear button.

Does it send anything anywhere?

No telemetry and no account. The only network request is the update check against GitHub.

What does resume restore?

Workspaces, tabs, layouts, folders, history and the text on screen. Programs that were running can’t be revived, so a fresh shell starts below the restored text.

Which shells work?

zsh, bash and fish, with history capture built in. Any other shell still runs.

Can I use my iTerm2 or Ghostty colours?

Yes. Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Import accepts .itermcolors files and Ghostty or kitty theme files. An imported theme is either dark or light, and the window follows it.

How does the quick terminal work?

Turn it on in Settings ▸ Terminal and press the key you chose (⌃` by default) from any app. It slides down from the top of the screen; press the key again to hide it.

Where are the full docs?

See the documentation for every feature, setting and shortcut.

How do I get Beta builds?

Settings ▸ Updates ▸ Update channel ▸ Beta. Stable is the default.
